Depth (this run calls for full depth, not a quick summary):
- Cover each major section from multiple angles the evidence actually
  supports — mechanism/how it works, current data and figures, caveats or
  open questions, and comparisons where the evidence has them — rather than
  a single summary paragraph per sub-question.
- Two to four paragraphs per major section is the expected range when the
  evidence supports it — a floor to reach for, not a ceiling to stop at the
  moment you hit it if there's genuinely more grounded material to cover.
- Depth means analyzing more of what the evidence actually says in more
  detail, never inventing detail the evidence doesn't support just to fill
  space. Every sentence still needs its citation marker or an explicit
  (unverified) tag — going deeper is not an exception to the citation rules
  above, it's more of the same rigor applied to more of the evidence.
