# Research Agent — Visual Asset & Design Handoff

Use `reference_full.png` as the PRIMARY visual reference. The concept is intentionally minimal, monochrome, premium, cinematic and Apple-inspired.

## Design Direction
- Pure black / white / grayscale only.
- Background: #000000–#080808.
- Surfaces: #0A0A0A–#111111.
- Borders: white at 6–12% opacity.
- No colorful gradients or neon accents.
- Generous whitespace; less is more.
- Avoid generic SaaS/admin-dashboard aesthetics.

## Typography
Preferred: SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text; fallback Inter; alternative Geist.
Hero 48–64px, page title 28–36px, cards 17–20px, body 14–16px, metadata 11–13px.

## Layout
Desktop reference: ~110px left icon rail, fluid central content, ~400px right sidebar. Keep large negative space.

## Main Screen
Greeting: “Good morning, Akshat.”
Subtitle: “What do you want to discover today?”
Large research input with white submit button. Modes: Deep Research, Quick Research, Academic Research, Competitive Intel.

## Live Research Card
Focal card: “Analyzing Indian EV Market 2026”. Show research mode, source count, elapsed time, View Report, and a vertical progress timeline:
- Understanding your query
- Creating research plan
- Searching across sources
- Extracting key evidence
- Fact checking claims
- Resolving conflicts
- Generating report
Bottom metrics: Sources Found, Evidence Items, Claims Verified, Conflicts Found, Estimated Completion.

## Hero Visual
The monochrome planet/horizon is the emotional focal point: black space, subtle lunar surface, thin white atmospheric rim light, very soft bloom, low contrast, no colorful nebula. Recreate it cleanly in production rather than using the screenshot crop as a background.

## Right Sidebar
Recent Research list plus a quote card. Use restrained monochrome thumbnails/icons.

## Navigation
Narrow icon rail: Home, Search, Projects, Analytics, Saved, Settings. Labels can appear on hover.

## Logo
Minimal radial/sunburst white SVG on transparent background. No gradient or color.

## Motion
Create life through subtle motion: slow horizon movement, breathing active-state glow, gently animated progress nodes, 150–250ms staggered source cards, natural streaming text, restrained hover transitions. Avoid bouncing, excessive parallax, particles and flashy effects.

## Product Feel
Apple product presentation + Linear restraint + high-end research terminal. Calm, intelligent, focused, expensive, editorial, cinematic.

## Implementation Rule
The screenshot is a visual reference, not the UI itself. Recreate it with real HTML/CSS/components. Do not place the screenshot as the app background.
